## 1. The Brand Identity Core

**PROFOUND** brings together enterprise software engineering, hardened cybersecurity, bespoke web flagships, answer engine optimization (AEO/GEO), brand architecture, and algorithmic performance creative under one unified global brand.

### The Identity Concept: The Architectural Aperture P
The primary symbol is built from two foundational elements:
1. **The Monolithic Pillar**: A grounded vertical mast symbolizing engineering rigor, zero-trust infrastructure, and enterprise endurance.
2. **The Geometric Loop & Aperture**: An intentional mathematical return featuring a vibrant **Electric Blue (`#1677FF`)** focal nexus, symbolizing discovery, optical depth, and forward possibility.

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing Rules

### Clear Space Zone
To ensure maximum visual impact, maintain a mandatory clear space around the logo equal to the width of the **P Pillar (`X`)**:
- Top, bottom, left, and right exclusion boundary: **1.0X**.
- No text, iconography, graphic lines, or background clutter may enter this boundary.

### Minimum Reproduction Sizing
- **Horizontal Logo**: Minimum digital display width is **120px**; minimum print width is **32mm**.
- **Standalone Symbol**: Minimum digital display size is **16×16px**; minimum print width is **6mm**.

## 5. Unacceptable Logo Alterations

To preserve brand equity and visual dignity:
1. **DO NOT** rotate, skew, or distort the symbol or wordmark.
2. **DO NOT** replace the Electric Blue (`#1677FF`) focal point with arbitrary colors.
3. **DO NOT** add drop shadows, outer glows, bevels, or 3D gradient fills.
4. **DO NOT** change the typeface or alter the letterspacing of the wordmark.
5. **DO NOT** place the dark logo on busy, high-contrast photographic backgrounds without an overlay.
6. **DO NOT** rearrange the relationship between the symbol and the wordmark outside of the approved horizontal and stacked lockups.
